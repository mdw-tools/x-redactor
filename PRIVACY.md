# Privacy policy

Redactor for X does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

- **Page content:** the extension reads the text of x.com and twitter.com pages inside your browser to find and censor profanity. That text never leaves your browser.
- **Settings:** your choices (on/off, mode, censor style and word list) are saved with Chrome's sync storage, so Chrome can sync them between your own signed-in browsers. The developer never receives them.
- **No network requests, analytics, tracking or remote code.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mdw-tools/x-redactor/issues.
