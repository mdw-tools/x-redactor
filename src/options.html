Word list

One entry per line, matched as a whole word regardless of case. Lines starting with # are comments. Use * for "any letters": damn* matches damned, *shit* matches bullshit.

Common workarounds are caught automatically: stretched letters (shiiit), look-alike characters (sh1t, a$$), spelled-out words (f.u.c.k, s h i t) and styled or disguised Unicode letters (𝐟𝐮𝐜𝐤, ｓｈｉｔ, fück).
